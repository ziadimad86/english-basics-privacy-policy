Privacy Policy — Crystal Valley

Effective date: November 10, 2026

About the game

Crystal Valley is a puzzle game by Ziad Imad (Android package: ziadimad.android.crystalvalley). It is intended for a general audience aged 13 and over. The game does not require an account or ask for your name, email address, contacts, or precise location.

Game data

Your saved game progress, rewards, and settings are stored on your device. We do not provide cloud saves or require a player account. If you allow optional usage analytics, gameplay events described below are sent to Google Analytics for Firebase.

Optional usage analytics

Usage analytics is off by default. If you choose Allow analytics, the game uses Google Analytics for Firebase to measure app sessions and engagement, level starts and results (level number, success, and stars), and the number of in-game rewards collected. The SDK also processes app-instance identifiers, app version, device and operating-system information, and approximate location derived from network information. These measurements help us understand player retention, improve the game, and evaluate advertising performance and revenue when linked with AdMob and Google Ads.

We do not send your name, email address, contacts, precise location, or a custom player account identifier in gameplay events. We disable advertising-ID collection and advertising-personalization consent for Firebase Analytics; AdMob advertising choices are handled separately as described below. Google processes Analytics data under its Privacy Policy. See also Firebase privacy information.

You can allow or turn off usage analytics in Settings → Usage analytics. Declining does not change gameplay or rewards. Turning it off stops future Analytics collection and resets Analytics data and identifiers stored locally by the SDK. This does not automatically delete data already sent to Google's servers. Contact us for questions or data-deletion requests; we may need information to identify the relevant data, and we do not collect your identity as part of analytics.

Advertising data

The game uses Google AdMob to display banner advertisements. The Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and share IP address, app and advertisement interactions, diagnostic information, and device identifiers such as the Android advertising ID and app set ID. Google uses this information for advertising, measurement, and fraud prevention. Advertising may be personalized where permitted by your choices and applicable requirements.

Google handles this information under the Google Privacy Policy. We do not sell your personal information. When required, the game asks for advertising consent before requesting ads and provides a Privacy choices control in Settings when available.

Security, retention, and deletion

Game progress and settings remain on your device until you clear the app's data or uninstall it. Data sent by the Google Mobile Ads SDK and Firebase Analytics is encrypted in transit. Analytics data is retained according to the Google Analytics property's retention settings; Google's other retention practices are described in its privacy policy. You can reset or delete the Android advertising ID in your device settings.

Children

Crystal Valley is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly request personal information from children.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy if the game or its data practices change. The effective date above will be updated. For privacy questions, contact ziadimad.android@gmail.com.
